01 / WHAT THIS PLACE IS

A bar that keeps
conjuring drinks

Jungle is where the bartenders try the ideas nobody else in Reykjavík is trying yet. The list moves. Something new is always halfway invented behind the bar.

„Jungle staðurinn til að smakka nýjar og framandi kokteilhugmyndir sem barþjónarnir þar eru stanslaust að galdra fram.“

Jakob Eggertsson, co‑owner MBL.IS · 5 SEPT 2024

Roughly: Jungle is the place to taste new and unfamiliar cocktail ideas, the ones the bartenders there are constantly conjuring up.

03 / THE PEOPLE UPSTAIRS

Mixed by
this lot

Exactly as they introduce themselves on jungle.is. Scroll to meet everyone.

  • Jónas

    WORLD CLASS BARTENDER OF ICELAND 2017

    „The guy in the fish shirt“, one of Iceland’s finest bartenders. You better have fun when he’s your waiter because he specifically requests it.

  • Víkingur

    BACARDI LEGACY ICELAND 2019

    Second place in World Class Bartender of the year 2019 after only two years behind a bar, and reached the global finals by knocking out Finland.

  • Jakob

    BROUGHT YOU COCOBUTT & STAIRWAY

    Bartender and social media manager, in charge of showing you their mistakes so you don’t have to make them yourself. Also a legend on the grill.

  • Svala

    THE LIFE OF THE PARTY

    She likes white wine. Nay, she loooves white wine. Probably their weirdest staff member, which is exactly why they love her. No bad vibes, homie.

  • Shaun

    GARNISH · KOMBUCHA · EVERYTHING

    A gardening, bartending, flower growing, kombucha brewing hero. Leaking sink? WHERE IS SHAUN? New garnish needed? SHAAAUN!?

  • Ólafur

    DJ ÓLI JUNGLE BOY

    Get a guy who can do both. Catch him on weekends trying to bartend and DJ at the same time, making sure they don’t only play techno from 2007.

  • Michał

    MEME LORD

    Probably the nicest person on the planet. If you don’t love him at first, you will love him eventually. Loves a good Jungle Bird. The cocktail. Not birds.

  • Oliver

    THE SLOVAKIAN STALLION

    MMA, football, weights, cocktail shaking, whatever, he does it. Loves his whisky, negronis and techno. Terrible at karaoke, but don’t tell him that.

04 / THE ARCHIVE

Drinks that have
passed through

Every one of these was on the Jungle list at some point and is not on it now. That turnover is the whole point of the place. Move your cursor and they follow you around.

    05 / THE ROOM

    Green, low and
    a floor above the street

    Laid back through the week, louder at the weekend, when the crew take turns DJing and pouring at the same time. Walk in, mostly. No reservations for small groups.

    06 / KOKTEILANÁMSKEIÐ

    Learn to make
    them yourself

    A two hour cocktail workshop at the bar, run by the people who write the list. Their own description, in their own words:

    1. 01

      Þið veljið 3 kokteila af seðlinum. Ef það eru einhverjir aðrir drykkir sem ykkur langar að prufa, ekki hika við að nefna það við okkur.

    2. 02

      Byrjum á léttum fordrykk og svo förum við yfir handtökin og lærum að hrista upp þessa geggjuðu kokteila.

    3. 03

      Geggjuð stemning og allir gera sína eigin drykki. Svo fáið þið allar uppskriftirnar sendar í pósti eftir námskeiðið.

    RUNS ABOUT 2 HOURS · BOOK AT JUNGLE@JUNGLE.IS
